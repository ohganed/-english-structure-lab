# Arabic Living World A1 — Coverage Audit

Status: 150 Experiences assembled in three batches.

## Covered living domains
- meeting people and greetings
- home and everyday objects
- food and drink
- shopping and payment
- city, directions and transport
- friends and family
- time and appointments
- body, health and basic help-seeking
- hotel and travel
- small problems and repair strategies
- preferences, feelings and weather
- phone/messages and independent daily planning
- simple past/future exposure and integrated everyday use

## Communicative coverage
The course includes greeting, introducing, asking who/where/when/how much, requesting, choosing, thanking, apologizing, locating objects, giving simple directions, ordering, paying, describing basic qualities, talking about family, arranging meetings, reporting illness, asking for help, reporting simple problems, expressing likes/dislikes and feelings, inviting/accepting/declining, and short past/future references.

## Structural coverage already present
- nominal sentences and adjective predicates
- basic verbal sentences
- pronouns and possessive suffixes
- demonstratives
- definite article
- common prepositions
- question words and yes/no questions
- negation with لا and ليس
- noun–adjective agreement exposure
- إضافة exposure
- direct objects and accusative exposure
- imperatives in practical situations
- present/imperfect verbs
- selected past forms
- future with سـ
- أن + imperfect exposure
- dual exposure in a high-context scene

## Pedagogical safeguards
- Situation precedes explanation.
- Audio can precede script.
- Arabic text is not treated as a translation quiz.
- Vowel marks are user-controlled and should appear only when the vowel-mark control is active.
- Analysis remains available underneath the experience rather than dominating the scene.
- Re-encounters are distributed across new situations instead of being labelled as review drills.

## Gaps / next refinement pass
1. Skill metadata is still broad. Each Experience should receive a primary skill and optional secondary skills instead of marking all four skills equally.
2. Character/place continuity needs explicit `characterMemory`, `placeMemory`, and `previousEventIds` links.
3. The Deep Analysis references currently declare Word Anatomy / Chunks / Sentence Architecture / I'rab availability, but full per-utterance analysis objects still need to be generated and linked.
4. A few late-A1 scenes intentionally expose structures above strict productive A1. These should be tagged `exposureOnly` where appropriate rather than treated as mastery targets.
5. Vocabulary/root/pattern frequency should be counted mechanically after the analysis layer is generated, then sparse areas should be filled with new situations rather than grammar drills.
6. Listening needs more speaker variation, environmental sound and short two-person exchanges.
7. Writing needs a dedicated progression from tracing/letter connection to short messages and practical forms.
8. Native iPhone haptics cannot be guaranteed from a web page; web vibration is attempted only when the browser supports it. A future native iOS wrapper can provide precise Core Haptics synchronization.

## Decision
A1 is now large enough for an end-to-end Living World trial. The next content step should be a refinement pass over A1 before mass-producing A2: add continuity links, skill weighting, full Structure Lab analysis, and a small number of missing situations found by mechanical coverage counts.
